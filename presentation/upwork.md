**Project description** (487/600)
```
Goal: detect AI-cloned and synthetic voices for fraud screening (calls, voice logins, uploads). Solution: I re-implemented AASIST (graph-attention anti-spoofing, ICASSP 2022) from scratch in PyTorch, plus LCNN and RawNet2 baselines, trained on ASVspoof 2019 LA (13 attack systems, 11 unseen). Impact: 1.40% equal error rate; catches 84% of AI clips while flagging only 0.1% of real voices; runs in-browser so audio never leaves the device. Demo: priyanshu-singh-git.github.io/voiceshield
```
