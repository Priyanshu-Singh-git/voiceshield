How it works

A graph-attention network (AASIST) reads the raw waveform: learnable band-pass filters, a residual encoder, then one graph over frequency bands and one over time frames, fused by a heterogeneous graph with a master node. It learns the tiny spectral and timing artefacts that vocoders leave behind.

Trained on ASVspoof 2019 LA (English read speech, 2019-era synthesis). Newer commercial voice-cloning tools can be harder to catch: treat this as a screening signal, not proof. Code, benchmark and limits.
