# VoiceShield deck style (reference from the user, 2026-10-02)

Target look: a modern "business proposal" deck. Drop the original reference screenshot into this folder as
`reference.png` if you have the file (the pasted image was not saved to disk).

## Palette
- Background: warm off-white / light grey, `#F2F2EF` (cards slightly lighter `#FAFAF8`)
- Ink: near-black `#111111` for headings and body; mid-grey `#6B6B6B` for small meta text
- Accent: **neon / acid yellow** `#E8F542` (used sparingly: one highlighted card, small badges, key numbers)
- Contrast cards: solid black `#111111` with white text (one per slide at most)

## Typography
- One geometric grotesk sans (Manrope / Inter / Segoe UI Variable style). **No script fonts.**
- Huge headings (60–80 pt), tight letter-spacing, 2-line titles with deliberate line breaks
  ("Business / -Proposal", "Introducing / Our Company", "A Vision Backed / by Clear Goals")
- Title can mix weights: first line light/regular, second line bold, or vice versa
- Tiny meta text at the top: brand/logo top-left, small label or page number top-right (8–10 pt)

## Layout and shapes
- Generous whitespace, asymmetric grid; title often bottom-left or top-left, very large
- **Large rounded-corner cards** (radius ~24–32 px at 1920 width), no borders, soft fill differences
- Numbered step cards: `01 02 03 04` in big type inside cards, one card yellow, one black, rest light
- Small **yellow square badge with a black asterisk** (`✳`) as a recurring motif in a card corner
- Pill-shaped tags/buttons (e.g. small "Learn more" chips), some yellow

## Imagery
- **Monochrome 3D abstract renders** (chrome/greyscale blobs, ribbons, glossy spheres, wavy forms) inside rounded frames
- Images are greyscale; colour comes only from the yellow accent
- For VoiceShield: greyscale waveforms / spectrograms rendered as 3D-looking abstract shapes fit this style

## Slide patterns seen in the reference
1. Cover: tiny tagline top-left, two key stats (e.g. `$180K`, `99%`), giant two-line title, dark 3D image strip, yellow `✳` badge
2. Agenda: four numbered cards 01–04, one yellow, one black
3. Intro: big 3D shape left, giant title right, short paragraph + pill button
4. Executive summary: title top-left, 3D image left, two text columns right, yellow badge
5. Proposed solution: stacked bullet cards with yellow highlights left, giant title right
6. Vision: 3D image top, giant two-line title bottom, black card with yellow `✳`
7. Market analysis: two cards (one yellow), giant title bottom-left, 3D image right
8. Product overview: giant title top-left, device/phone mock-up right, small feature list with yellow tags

## Rules carried over
- Slide 1 is the Upwork thumbnail: product name huge + one USP line + one strong visual
- Every slide also exported as its own 1920×1080 PNG + PDF, from a real .pptx
- Positive, confident phrasing (no "not cherry-picked" style lines)
